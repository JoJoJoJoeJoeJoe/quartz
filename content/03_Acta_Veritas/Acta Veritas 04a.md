## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

Needing to fulfill my oath to the Scions of Hope Church, the warband was sent to the underhive on an intelligence operation. This report articulates the information [[Adami]] secured. Adami met Famulous Sister [[Ariana Joy]]. There, Sister Joy was searching for leads on a Sister down in the underhive assisting the local population ([[Sister Thalia]]), but she also possessed information and knowledge of Lord [[Iric Advanis]] (the Scion). Adami offered an exchange: for Sister Joy to assist our search for the Scion and for us to assist her search for this mysterious Sister.

Sister Joy revealed rebels snuck up to the upper hive and came back down in a hurry, indicative that they kidnapped Iric Advanis. Yet, near the forge exhausts, there was a commotion once they came down; this was when Advanis escaped captivity. From there, Gangers have sought after Advanis, seeking the reward accompanying such a bounty.

This is what Adami learned separately from the other two. What [[Evodine]] and [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] learned is covered in [[Acta Veritas 04b]].

**Warband Rejoined.**

Sister Joy advised that the old transit system for ore was the best place to begin the search with the information gathered thus far. This concludes Acta Veritas 04a & 04b.
