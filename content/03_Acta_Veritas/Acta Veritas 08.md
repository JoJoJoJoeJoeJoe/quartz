## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

In the box, catalogued in file [[Acta Veritas 07]], I found the [[Arch-Sorcerer|Deceiver]]’s symbol -- [[The Eye|the Eye]] that sees, knows, changes; the Eye that understands, answers; the Eye that wants to know if you want to know. [[Arch-Sorcerer|The Deceiver]], I know, embodies sorcery, knowledge, and change; He offers the flame to burn everything away and remake it. This, I knew immediately, was the force which twisted the ideology of the followers of the Star Saint.

Then, soon after, the company retired for the night. In the midst of my slumber, I experienced a vivid dream. I walked along a dark corridor until I came before a door. You were there to caution me, and said the best course of action -- at times -- was not to know. You asked if I was certain, and feeling that I was, I stepped through. On the other side, an entity of your likeness asked for my report. I answered it, telling it what little I knew at the time, before requesting to see the Deceiver’s mark. I handed it over and it tossed aside the symbol, claiming it to be little more than scrap, and then requested [[Charm]].

That is when I exposed the [[Deceiver's Spawn|daemon]] imitating your likeness. It offered to answer any of my questions; I answered it with roiling fire and repelled the daemon from my mind, before it had the chance to influence my mind overtly. It ran, swearing upon vengeance, multi-colored eyes breaking out across its form.

When I woke, I discovered the Deceiver’s mark had vanished without a trace. [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] told me there were eyes on the mark before I slept -- he wasn’t wrong on that. This does expose a daemon active in the affairs of Initeium, not solely a heretical cult.
