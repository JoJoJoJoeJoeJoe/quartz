## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

Sister [[Verina Astall]] alerted me of [[Elvan]]’s absence as the penitent congregants returned in the morning. This prompted an investigation with [[Horker]], a local resident, as my guide. We journeyed thusly to his hab: Room 118. While Horker beset himself on unlocking Elvan’s door, I investigated the area and found a broken grate that led to a sluice, but this was a dead end. However, there were stains and signs that something crawled out from the piping. Sister Astall informed me that rebels were known to use the tunnels as a means of getting through and across the hive, suggesting the tracks may have been from those times. The interior of Elvan’s residence presented nothing abnormal nor worthy of note.

Instead, Horker took me on the path Elvan would have used on his way to the church. There, we found a maintenance hole exposed by artillery shelling. After conversing how to go forward from there, Horker was led back to the church to continue his duties and I flagged [[Neyor]] to come over. Once he joined my company, we entered the maintenance tunnels accompanied by Sister Astall’s servo-skull.

On our way there, we encountered a small robed mutant (prime mutation: third arm) but instead of pursuing it as it fled, I detected two individuals wearing rebel armor behind us. They were moving boxes of grenades down a smaller passageway from the maintenance tunnel, obviously transporting them elsewhere en masse. I called for their arrest, they resisted, and I pursued. Instead of letting one of the rebels get away, I threw a grenade at them which set off a chain-reaction.

Once we got a safe distance away, I interrogated the remaining, but dying, rebel. I was able to convince him to tell me what he saw of Elvan: him, walking alone late at night, “going somewhere.” Unfortunately, the rebel perished before questioning could proceed further.

I reconvened with Sister Astall and we went over the discoveries. She informed me of her intent to hail the Militant Sisters to bring greater security to the hab, and I voiced my assent.
