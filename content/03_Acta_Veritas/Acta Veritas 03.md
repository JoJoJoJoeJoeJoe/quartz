## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

The congregants made their neighborhood aware of the rations available at the Church in the morning, but gangers interfered in their duties. Sister Astall guided me to them and I dispensed with the gangers with the assistance of Dialogus Sister [[Evodine]], who I met on the scene. She acted on behalf of the congregants and protected them. However, by the way one ganger was reduced to paste and how the shrapnel markers extended in all directions away from Evodine, I quickly pieced together that she was a psyker. Before either of us could converse at any greater length, I was recalled back to the church and Evodine -- who was going there already -- followed.

On arrival, we came across a dozen or so gangers firing upon [[Verina Astall |Sister Astall]]. A kriegsman entered the fray with us -- Guardsman [[24601-331-Draeta|24601-331-Draeta (Lucky)]] -- in the midst of combat. Afterwards, Lucky informed me of his orders, a year outdated, which attached him to my command -- straight from Segmentum Obscurus Command. There is no clause or wording within his orders that enables me to redirect the kriegsman to the local regiment, and so he now serves with me.

Sister Astall alerted us that gangers were striking en masse from the lower hives and creating a proper riot, which tied the hands of the local Astra Militarum regiment, enforcers, and the PDF. In their stead, we acted. Sister Astall’s servo-skull led Evodine, Lucky, and myself to the ganger hold-out while she stayed back as a result of her critical injuries. As we initiated our assault on the hold-out, we encountered the deathcultist Assassin [[Adami]] (I believe you know of her, Lord [[Inquisitor Somtum|Inquisitor]]). She joined forces with us, and we continued deeper within the hold-out.

[[Groxhound]], as per your orders, was slain by Adami. In the ensuing combat, Evodine -- in a display of terrible power -- crushed multiple crates containing brutish mutants that out-rival me in size. At the battle’s conclusion, one man stood alone in our captivity: a ganger by the name of Neyor. The interrogation that followed is documented in file [[AV-03 Field Interrogation]], with the main finding accusing noble houses and the Ecclesiarchy of purchasing smuggled mutants. In exchange for his life, he led us to a nearby ganger safehouse.

Upon arrival, we found the gangers guarding the safehouse slain. The perpetrator of this aggression was a brutish mutant with a bomb collar. This mutant was slain, with effort, moments before the bomb detonated. In the wake of the destruction, I extinguished the flames to preserve as much of the mutant specimen as possible. From it, Adami extracted samples and collected an intact spleen. The results of the tests run on the sample can be found in file [[AV-03 Verispex Analysis]].
