## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

Needing to fulfill my oath to the Scions of Hope Church, the warband was sent to the underhive on an intelligence operation. They went to a brothel named ‘The Imperium’s Touch,’ led by [[Adami]]. This report articulates the information [[Evodine]] and [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] secured. In a conversation with one of the brothel’s prostitutes, it was learned that men of [[House Advanis]]’ rival recently completed a raid against them and were celebrating in the brothel. The prostitute also commented that a new wave of thrones were circulating in the underhive: mercenaries hired, arms bought and smuggled, and other preparatory actions.

Their informant also revealed a lot of movement to the fringes of the Underhive. She’s heard of squatters in the old Custodian Block, and about a woman preaching the [[Saint of Stars |Star Saint]]’s doctrine ([[Thalia]]).

This is the extent of the information retrieved from the prostitute.
