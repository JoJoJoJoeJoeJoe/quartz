## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

Once [[Verina Astall|Sister Astall]] was escorted to her fellow Sisters, we were directed to Scribe [[Dalen]]’s office. There, [[Zendros Vayle|Zendros]] connected to the database and discovered a hostile connection planted within that has allowed the users of this connection to observe through the servitors along with the information digitally recorded. The terminal at Scribe Dalen’s office is connected to Requisition Hub ML-1421 (Mechanicus Manufacturing Loading Dock), where we presume the hostile connection can be traced back to, something we made the Sisters of Battle aware of before the completion of this objective. Furthermore, Zendros discovered trace amounts of higher executive functioning in at least one of the servitors -- it wanted to listen in, protect ‘the afflicted’ and had done so by making ‘a deal,’ he told me.

At [[Evodine]]’s counsel I placed Dalen under arrest, left Remi to guard him, and conversed privately with the company. Evodine read the consciousness and subconsciousness of Scribe Dalen and found he was deliberately placing forms in the rejection pile and marking high priority messages as low priority, except he was unaware as to why. She found his mind was protected by outside influence, the same influence that was targeting the Scribe. I conferred with [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]], whose psychically enhanced senses revealed this was not the work of genestealers but something else -- something like what he felt at the Bastion Courtyard. Evodine suggested that if this was the work of sorcery or a psyker serving the archenemy, they would have left something akin to a totem behind -- and so began our search.

Zendros uncovered notes to a noble within the Scribe’s office and observed an unsettling pattern (he did not clarify too much on this); similarly, he found a communique to the Mechanicus for mechanisms damaged during the rebellion that were now deliberately delayed. More importantly, Evodine uncovered pieces of parchment with one-off marks on them hidden throughout the building.

Arranging the marks, like a cursed jigsaw puzzle, I put together The Eye. I saw it blink, then the eye focused on me, and I felt its presence pressing against my mind. A flame manifested where the pupil was. While I was frozen in place, Lucky stepped over and crumpled the Eye. From it, I felt traces of power from something or someone very powerful -- either from a sorcerer or a mark given by a daemon. Before the presence could leave wholly, I tracked its presence back down to the Depths whereas Evodine was able to track it to the uppermost levels of the hive, the Depths, and one other place she couldn’t discern. Declaring the area as a psychic hazard, I contacted the Sisters and summoned them and sent away everyone else but Zendros.

In the end, an Insidiant Sister was left to scour the office and we brought Celestian Superior [[Magadal Greer]] to the Scribe. She sent him to the Sisters Dialogus for observation and penance. Dalen, before being sent away, told us he observed an out-of-place hooded woman, clumsy, that spilled a box of parchment who we imagined to either be Thena or [[Isolde]]. This concluded our investigation on the Scribe’s Office and marked our preparations for descending into the Depths a second time.
