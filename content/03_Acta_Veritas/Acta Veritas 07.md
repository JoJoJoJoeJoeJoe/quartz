## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

Following the completion of our previous objective, rescuing [[Iric Advanis]] from captivity, we initiated the search for the Sister that [[Ariana Joy]] was searching for. Iric opted to join our retinue and assist in our efforts, and so he followed along.

On the way through the Stricken Quarter, [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] observed shadows moving unnaturally through a hab unit. Upon entry, we noticed this unit had stairs -- an abnormal feature for a building so low in the Hive. Lucky inspected it, shortly followed by [[Evodine]]. Before long, we heard shouting from the sublevel. [[Adami]] and I took the stairs down, yet we found they were never-ending -- we encountered the ward of a sorcerer who was attempting to obscure the sublevel from our gaze. Evodine forcefully dispelled the shroud and we rejoined. Lucky reported they found charred skeletons fighting over a chest they found, one which they brought with them. Having sensed something foul and suspecting some sort of trap, the company did not open it. As the individual that has the most experience with chaos (and the largest), I was entrusted with watching over the box. *(Addendum: Further interaction with the box is documented in file [[Acta Veritas 08]].)*

After this incident, we resumed our search. We encountered a habblock bearing imagery of the Star Saint’s symbol, the same symbol used by some of the rebel cells. Before the gate, we elected to send in Ariana, Adami, and Evodine -- all of whom were less menacing and conspicuous than Iric, Lucky, and myself. During their expedition, they encountered Sister Thalia and began to assist the ailing within the Church.

In the meantime, we happened upon a man ([[Panct Ward]]) beating a woman (Harriet) -- civilians. I arrested the man and separated the woman from the group, supervised by Lucky. After I separated the two, Panct Ward was quick to reveal that Harriet had involvement regarding or participated in the cannibalization of his shiftmate, Techas. He described Techas as behaving strangely ever since courting Harriet: overt displays of affection and obsession, following her odd whims such as repairing broken objects to seemingly no end, all for a month or so. When Ward checked in on Techas, he found Harriet ravenously eating meat, unconcerned about Techas. I investigated their hab unit and discovered remnants of meat as well as a blood trail leading into one of the air vents, paired with bloody handprints of anything from a small woman to an average sized man’s -- from three different individuals. Meaning, Harriet had assistance.

When I returned to the others and interrogated Harriet, her sole concern was with leaving and escaping. Whether this was a natural inclination or from her infection, I cannot say. However, through Evodine’s psychic intervention, the influence from Harriet’s mind was cleared. She has been placed in Sister Thalia’s custody and supervision.

We decided to stake out at the building while our more covert members, that being Iric, Adami, Ariana, and Evodine, went inside. Lucky warded off four adolescent would-be gangers and turned them away from the building through a ruse, but later disclosed to me he suspected two of them were infected. Adami and Ariana met enemy contact in the ventilation system and successfully killed a psychic-active genestealer mutant and one that appeared to be an adolescent. We retrieved the names of accomplices, following: Devis, Redan, and Detsa (one of these three are deceased), Bents (a large brute), and [[Thena]] (a woman donning a heavy hood).

Lucky found and captured Redan, who so boldly proclaimed ‘they’ would topple the spires. Evodine interrogated and then purged Redan of influence. So, too, was Redan given to the custody of Sister Thalia.

Iric pointed out a potential informant, ‘[[Old Crank|The Old Crank]],’ who we visited. He revealed to us that there was a layer deeper than where we stood and that ‘they’ (genestealers) use the dysfunctional breath recycler as a concealed access point. He granted us old schematics of the local area (see [[AV-07 Verispex Analysis]], Item 03). After that, we escorted two cooperative locals to Sister Thalia’s congregation; however, along the way, the bulk of our party was drawn to the sound of a firefight.

The aftermath of the firefight left one ganger dead on the spot, felled by the Arbitrator [[Zendros]] accompanied by Enforcer [[Kaytia Nintos]]. He was sent our way by [[Aurora Harpina|Lieutenant Harpina]] on the pretense that we can assist one another in our agendas. For the Arbitrator, he seeks to restore Initeium’s capability to restore tithes while we seek to purge it of its corruption. We were fast allies and joined together. With the matter settled, we all -- at last -- went through Hope’s Gate and entered the community Sister Thalia has banded together.

The air-scrubbers were working in this community, it was well-taken care of, but was guarded by individuals who wore the colors of Initeium’s rebels. In spite of this, they were co-operative and docile. Soon after our entry was when I, personally, first met Sister Thalia. She is a comely woman with auburn hair and forgoes any material ostentatious presentation. She provided the company with recaf, rations, and a place to rest. During our conversation with her, there was interpersonal conflict, which caused the company to split -- leaving Sister Thalia and I alone. The contents of our conversation can be found in [[AV-07 Field Interrogation]].

Overall, our activities led us to uncovering more genestealer presence in the population. In particular, it affirms our suspicions that the infection heightens libido with sexual intercourse being a potential vector for infection as well. If I were to make a guess, I imagine infected males are fed to the mutant spawn of infected females in order to provide sustenance to the spawn.

On the matter of this ‘Sister in the Underhive,’ I do not believe Sister Thalia’s actions will cause any interference in our investigation. To the contrary, I believe she will be a stabilizing force that will shed His light down there.
