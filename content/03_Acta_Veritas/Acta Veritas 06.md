## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

The warband, with [[Ariana Joy|Sister Joy]], joined me at the Lower Hive outpost. There, Sister [[Evodine]] informed me of her (former) rank of Dialogus, paired with the mutual exchange of information of each party’s exploits (documented in files [[Acta Veritas 04a]], [[Acta Veritas 04b]], and [[Acta Veritas 05]]). Then, tapping into latent psychic ability, [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky (Guardsman 24601-331-Draeta)]] divined the presence of the enemy within Trooper [[Oliver]]. Evodine, utilizing telepathy, removed the enemy’s presence from Trooper Oliver. [[Adami]] took a sample from Oliver for later testing, and when Oliver demonstrated illness-like symptoms, discerned his body was reacting as if it were purging a parasite and the delirium reminded her of an addict suffering withdrawal. Sister Joy was able to excuse this psychic event by playing it off as Evodine exorcising Oliver through faith and miracle. In the end, my hand was forced into revealing to Sergeant [[Fumical]] -- in private -- that I was there as part of an investigation you ordered, [[Inquisitor Somtum|Inquisitor]].

Evodine’s telepathic analysis of Oliver revealed two things of note. One, Oliver’s cognition was modified and suffered from hypersexuality that demonstrated itself as a heightened awareness and fixation on women. Elaboration on the sample Adami took from Trooper Oliver is located in file [[AV-06 Verispex Analysis]] (Item 01). Journeyman [[Samson]] revealed Oliver had a lover in the underhive, who we have suspected to be the vector of infection. Two, the presence formerly connected to Oliver was vast, inhuman, and traced to underhive -- perhaps further than that. After concluding our investigation at the gate, we resumed our search for the [[Iric Advanis|Scion]] of [[House Advanis]].

Our search brought us within an old rail line system. We encountered a trail of slain gangers before running into a mixed group of rebels and gangers hired-on as adjuncts. This battle ended in no casualties on our side. One surviving member was seized and questioned, the results of which can be found in file [[AV-06 Field Interrogation]]. The results of this interrogation affirm they were looking for the Scion.

We followed the trail of dead gangers who pursued the Scion. Before our next encounter, we found three ganger corpses. One was felled by precise lacerations, the other by blunt force trauma, and the last by a self-inflicted cut to the throat. The last I suspect, now, to have been from psychic influence from one of the [[Coraptor Hominis|genestealer]] mutants. Soon thereafter, we came across another genestealer mutant; however, this one was articulate and -- Emperor forgive me -- bordered on sapience. Upon revealing its ruse, it became violent, and another conflict began.

This battle, involving an odd dozen of gangers, the articulate mutant, and feral genestealer mutants stored in crates, all ended in zero casualties for the warband. During the battle, we united with Iric Advanis. After we ensured he required no sustenance nor medicae, and proper introductions were given, it was revealed to us that Ariana Joy and Iric Advanis have a close friendship with one another.

From these events, it is safe to presume the genestealer cult is pitting forces against House Advanis. A potential ally, or asset, to use against Initeium’s scourge.
