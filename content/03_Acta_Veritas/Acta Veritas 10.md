## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

Utilizing the schematics ([[AV-07 Verispex Analysis]], Item 03) given to us by the Old Crank in file [[Acta Veritas 07]], we entered the depths of Initeium. We noticed foot traffic of an average sized individual on our arrival and pursued its trail. This trail led us to a damaged servitor which, upon examination, revealed it was hunting a mutant and was the property of [[Metasurgeon Nu-Rho]]. Furthermore, it was revealed that higher executive functions were present in the servitor, and so we euthanized the servitor to spare it of any further suffering and indignity.

In the location where the servitor led us to, we uncovered a piece of parchment ([[AV-10 Verispex Analysis]], Item 01) which read: ‘One life, twice born, thrice sight.’ When we ascended, we soon ran into writing on the walls which [[Evodine]] translated for us: ([[AV-10 Verispex Analysis]], Item 02) ‘From highest to lowest, power to weakness, divinity to desecration. Change.’ As pointed out by Evodine, we found the warehouse that once housed the heretical texts [[Isolde]] threw over the nearby pits ([[AV-09 Field Interrogation 02]]).

We then pursued a trail of blood, identified as human. Along the way, utilizing their metaphysical senses, [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] and Evodine located a presence within the nearby warehouse. An all-encompassing assault was launched on the warehouse, covering all flanks, which led to the conflict against a Genestealer Elite Brute equipped with telepathic abilities, multiple arms, and high combat acuity. During this engagement, a link was established between its mind and my own. The thoughts I pulled from it were:

> *‘Threat: advanced giant. Relocate, cripple giant, eliminate seventy-five percent flesh. Return deep.’*

Once it detected the link, it thought:

> *‘Another. Useful. Targets identified.’*

This conflict ended in no deaths for the warband and our first meeting with Metasurgeon Nu-Rho. Our discussion with the Metasurgeon can be found in file [[AV-10 Field Interrogation]]. In summary, Nu-Rho identified the corpse as belonging to a xenos infiltrator vanguard species, [[Coraptor Hominis|Coraptor Hominis: Genestealers]]. Note: They showed surprise when [[Zendros|Zendros]] shared he detected higher brain functions within the Metasurgeon’s dysfunctional servitor.

Along the way back to our base, we encountered a series of ghost signals on the auspex and something lingering just on the edges of our periphery. [[Adami]] scouted ahead and found a lone surviving ganger, Grouse, whom she has claimed as a penitent.

At Support Strut iC-96421, the auspex revealed a winding path downwards. Similarly, Lucky’s senses detected a vast hostile presence readying itself for our arrival. I made the decision to better arm ourselves before our next excursion. This concluded our first foray into the Depths of Initeium.
