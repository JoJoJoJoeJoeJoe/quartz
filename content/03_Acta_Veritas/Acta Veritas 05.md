## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

Proceeding from the events of [[Acta Veritas 04]], I was in the process of rejoining my company when I was beset upon by some forty-odd assailants. Few wielded bladed weaponry, others with catch-poles, and most others with improvised weapons. The end of this encounter resulted in all of their deaths, with not a single one fleeing nor making a single sound -- not even as they burnt to death on the promethium trail I lit, riddled them with bullets, or cut into their flesh.

Shortly after the battle’s conclusion, two patrolling troopers hailed me. Amongst their company was Journeyman [[Samson]] and Guardsman [[Oliver]], a Guardsman who once served with [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]], and I invoked our mutual connection to help ease their concerns and verify my identity. After they guided me back to their bastion, I conversed with their commanding officer: Sergeant [[Fumical]].

During our conversation, Fumical revealed Lieutenant [[Aurora Harpina]] ordered no solitary patrol units on suspicion of underhive unrest; evidently, she was partly correct. Further, Fumical disclosed he’s been having difficulty securing equipment -- vox and munitions in particular ([[Acta Veritas 11]]) -- and attributed it to the upperhive’s scribes. In the interest of getting samples, he sent out three men to collect some corpses from the forty I killed, but they returned empty handed -- all of the bodies were already cleared out.

That concludes the end of this encounter, which only opened up more questions and didn’t provide very many answers. Those forty-odd civilians that pursued me -- were they bewitched or mutants?
