## II. OBJECTIVE

The elimination and purge of the Rebel Insurgents.

---

## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

I awoke half-dead after the conclusion of the battle and after the incendiary charges were set by the Tech-Adepts. During the battle and in the midst of investigating the rebel base, I came across mutants (that killed indiscriminately), which I now know to be [[Coraptor Hominis|Genestealer Mutants]]. One such mutant was stowed away in an opaque crate, like others I would come across in the future.

Within the rebel base, I encountered the lab of a biologis heretek. Dozens of bodies, salvaged for parts, laid in a recess underchamber with testing equipment surrounding the perimeter and an operating table in the center. This was when the rebel reinforcement, alone, stepped into the base. Upon confrontation, the rebel earnestly professed a lack of awareness to this cell’s heretekical actions, and was driven off in an ensuing firefight.

Afterwards, I remained and ensured the incendiary charges detonated before taking my leave. This concluded my part in the operation.
