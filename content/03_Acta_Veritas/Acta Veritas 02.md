## II. OBJECTIVE

The Scions of Hope was a tiny church from which an odd offshoot of standard Imperial Creed had taken hold. Ostensibly, it was in accordance with the Ecclesiarchy's many rules and regulations though it held fairly radical, frowned upon, views. Examples include 'Hope' being a core tenant, a close tie to the doctrine of Mercy to an extreme, and a level of forgiveness and kindness given to those that are undeserving in the Ecclesiarchy's eyes.

After the burning of the church, it was marked for reclamation by local Ecclesiarchal forces as to stabilize the damaged hab block it resided in. However multiple eye-witnesses report that within its ruins apparent figures could be seen in its ruins at odd hours. A proper investigation, before the Ecclesiarchy fully restores the church, is advised as to ensure proper security for this area of the habblock.

## III. NARRATIVE SUMMARY

I observed, on arrival, four adolescents yelling at the church doors for [[Verina Astall|Sister Astall]]’s servo-skull to leave. They despaired at Father Gacobson’s death and accused the Ecclesiarchy, among other institutions, of inaction and apathy. They fled upon my approach, and afterwards I climbed in through the Church window and met with the Sister’s servo-skull. Afterwards, I investigated the church and discovered a large store of rations in the basement. This was when I met Dialogus Sister [[Verina Astall]], who I informed I was acting on behalf of an old comrade to secure this place for the Ecclesiarchy and determine whether or not there was a rebel presence stalking the church at night. The Sister was displeased that the Ecclesiarchy hadn’t been informed of the eye-witness testimonials but found my cover agreeable.

Then, a while after I continued my investigation, the pounding at the doors resumed. Behind them were four civilians, three armed with makeshift melee armaments and one with a firearm, were there. I talked them down and, for their transgressions against one of His servants, tasked them with the restoration of the church and daily distribution of rations to their community under my supervision. In doing so, I am reintegrating the community into the Imperium’s institutions and founding a network for my assignments.

After I concluded my work with the penitents, [[Verina Astall |Sister Astall]] found scripture of prayer in the westward wing written upon the ground. She explained they were in High Gothic and spoke of a modified version that strayed from the proper interpretation of the Imperial Cult. Namely, because the writing espoused Hope as being a tenet, despite Ecclesiarchy denouncing Hope. However, when tested for heresy utilizing a sanctified candle, the wax revealed the text wasn't heretical. By literal definition, it is, but the wax suggests there is nothing impure about it. I posited that, if the [[Saint of Stars|Star Saint]] does exist, would her presence sanctify these writings? Sister Astall denied the idea, but I heard her at night later debate its legitimacy to herself -- uncertain.

During my time with the penitents, [[Ulys]] -- known for his strong faith -- said he’s felt a presence in the Church ever since the night of Father Gacobson’s death, but could not place it. As I discovered, Ulys’ beliefs were not unfounded.

My first encounter with psychic phenomenon was with the statue of the Star Saint, who appeared to me as flesh and bone for only a moment. That was when her title entered my mind: ‘Saint of Stars.’ Later, I was led by an invisible force -- playful and childlike -- to a prayer icon on the ground (Saint of Stars Prayer Icon -- see [[AV-02 Verispex Analysis]]). A holy spirit is connected to this item, one I have named Charm, who is -- themself -- connected to the Star Saint. Charm communicates to me through base feelings that almost constitute thoughts, counseling me in accordance with the Star Saint’s ideology.

My third psychic encounter, my second encounter with [[Charm]], was when I slept in the Scions of Hope Church. I awoke within a dream, in a world basking in soothing starlight. There, the statue of the Star Saint was gone and the incandescent silhouette of a child -- the holy spirit, [[Charm]] -- introduced themself to me, mute as they were. [[Charm]] confirmed they are the subject covered in the eye-witness statements. In addition to this, they told me they are connected to the Star Saint (which means [[Charm]] must tangentially be connected to the Imperator), and questioned me at length about my troubles and woes. That was when [[Charm]] revealed I, specifically, have been chosen by the [[Saint of Stars]] for some unknown purpose.

Altogether, the conclusion of this mystery can be answered thusly: the rebel activity consisted of disgruntled locals, and the disturbances spotted at night were the result of a holy spirit. Weren’t expecting that one, were you?
