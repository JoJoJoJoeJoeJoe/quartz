> **PRIORITY:** LOW

### 2. Missing Persons Near Low-Hive

This datafile holds a variety of hastily done enforcer reports of workers who failed to report for duty, a party of three missing young Ecclesiarchal acolytes, and a singular guardsman who failed to report in on patrol. Not much linked the groups other than the fact they went missing in the lower hive closer to the underhive inside the Forge sector. Enforcer documents believe this related to ganger activity. Perhaps reprisal attacks or 'example' making. They did not have the resources to investigate beyond the preliminary assessment.

The Inquisitorial documents noted that the workers who failed to report were in industrial and support occupations such as forge workers or menials tasked with repairing the damage to the hive after the uprising. The Inquisitor noted that these workers could be noted as more skilled than the average hiver. Not enough of these workers were taken to make a difference in work, however. The Inquisitor also noted that those taken had few connections with others or were otherwise isolated from the populace. This was noted as a concern for investigation mainly for the guardsman that was taken.

In total, a mere 135 people were listed as missing that fit the criteria here with a likely 20-30 as directly related.

*[[Evodine]] has noted there seems to be a preference for the female sex in the missing.*

The guardsman was one Corporal Tevas Olvan. Vendolan. Noted as being a sole survivor of his squad and, at the time of his taking, had not been attached to another squad. His personality is noted as serious and diligent and was his old squad's grenadier.

The three acolytes were the sisters (biological) Yoana and Wilma along with an older acolyte Tekas. They were on a trip through the forge to bring supplies to one of the few chapels in the Forge sector not devoted to the Mechanicus. They seemed to have almost disappeared into thin air as it was discovered that their supplies were right there along their pilgrim path. Timeframe for the investigations were the past four months, linking with the counter-insurgency time frame in Initeium. It was theorized however this could have been happening earlier but no documentation existed at that time.

---

### 3. Disappearance of Imperial Guard Equipment

The majority of items missing were common enough. Meal kits, water tanks, and similar sustenance items. Interspersed with this were the occasional notes about seemingly siphoned promethium from generator stores. Primary theory at the moment was black market dealings or guardsmen selling the material for local currency.

More questionable losses were las pistols and las guns though these were listed as 'resolved' with punishments listed as given out accordingly. The most eye-catching item lost was a case of explosives listed as defunct or in other terms, 'duds'.

Persons of interest listed to interview and investigate were Lieutenant Harpina and Lieutenant Rothas of the Cadians. Both were listed as having done investigations of the matter though Rothas seemed to handle the majority of the investigation. The Quartermaster Thomas of the firebase in the lower hives was also noted as having potential insight to the situation as he was the one to note the minor loss of promethium.

---

### 4. Highborn Rumor Mill Anomalies

This file contains a number of third party intercepted communiques, transcribed letters, and general hubbub. Listed as noteworthy by the Inquisitor was a change in 'proclivities' by one of the more degenerate noble houses ([[House Cormo'luyb]]) respective to their bed partners. They didn't seem to care about stock or nobility, at least as much since the rebellion was put down. It was still well within 'acceptable' behavior for a noble house, but the change happened quickly which caught the attention of the Inquisitor. He also linked this as possibly or perhaps tangentially related to the missing people.

Other rumors that were intercepted also seemed to hint at the possibility that a noble scion of a house was taken. [[House Advanis]] closed itself off from the other houses and only its leader showed himself at only the necessary gatherings. Along with this, their guards had been seen patrolling the upper hives. A tapped vox communique to the guard also indicated they asked for some guardsmen to patrol specific locations in the lower hive as if to look for someone or something.
