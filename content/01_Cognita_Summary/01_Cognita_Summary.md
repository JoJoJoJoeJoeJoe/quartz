## II. INQUEST SYNOPSIS

> **EXECUTIVE BRIEF**
> Thus far there has been: Confirmed Genestealer Cultists (Coraptor Hominis) activity and presence, evidence of heretical cultists, confirmed heretek presence, and an encounter with a Daemon of the Deceiver. Initeium is beset upon by enemies from within, without, and beyond.

	Finisculum Prime’s cursed narrative is not fully in frame, but my working belief goes as follows: Visionaries of the Saint of Stars adopted her interpretation of the Ecclesiarchy’s Scripture, one with a fixation on hope, justice, and mercy and initiated a community-building effort. Then, a presence (the Daemon of the Deceiver) began to infect the ideology with talk of ambition, change through force, and remaking of the old inciting followers of the founding visionaries into violence with the material support of Finisculum Prime’s nobility. The Genestealer Cult has been laying in wait for the past 25-30 years (minimum) and are contesting with the heretics for control over Initeium.

	In the aftermath of Finisculum Prime’s rebellion, gangers and extant rebel cells have been employed to distribute genestealer mutant brutes covertly throughout all levels of Initeium. On the other hand, recent activity of any heretical cultists have not been discovered, save for the Gellar Field failure aboard Guardsman 24601-331-Draeta’s transport and my encounter with the Deceiver’s Spawn.
