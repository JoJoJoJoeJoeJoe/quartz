> **RECORDING DEVICE:** Dataslate

## I. TRANSCRIPT

> **JOVE:** "Adami did pose a fair question, Sister. I am not that much of a fool -- I see the marks of rebels, former or otherwise, in your congregation. How can you assure me of the purity of their spirits?"
>
> **JOVE:** *(Sarcastically.)* "I hold the authority of the Imperium." *(Pauses.)* "I think you misunderstand me. I am trying to discern how you will show others, particularly higher authorities, that certain congregants are not a threat."
>
> **THALIA:** "Does authority indicate purity? I understood you, Sergeant. And the answer is there is no answer -- at least none that authority will accept. My congregation wears the colors of the sick and feeble, the garb of the dissenter, and the ideas of the first rebels that were subverted and broken. We have no way to show our purity except through our actions and our faith in Him."
>
> **JOVE:** "You're correct. I don't think anything, short of results, would stay their hand. One suggestion, however..." *(Pauses.)* "Change the appearance of the uniforms. Something that speaks to the idea of 'Hab-Block Watch'."
>
> **THALIA:** "I will try to impress it upon them. They hold to small embers of pride however. Now then, may I ask what brought you here?"
>
> **JOVE:** "Before I answer your question, it'd be better to answer one of mine. Have you noticed anything amiss around this community of yours? Or the people just outside of it?"
>
> **THALIA:** "You have quite the habit of diverting answers, Sergeant. Nay though, I have not. Those that step through Hope's Gate and share watch and protection with myself maintain themselves well. Though I do regret that my reach is small right now -- only those that come do I watch. I hope to change that soon however. Some hands and people have said that the underhive grows more restless, more violent as gangers seem to gather resources beyond their means. The veteran souls who walked with the rebels say it was like that before too, material given by unknown sources." *(Refers to [[House Cormo'luyb|House Cormo]])*
>
> *(RECORD: Paused.)*
> *(RECORD: Resume.)*
>
> **THALIA:** "You still haven't said what brought you here. Only what moves your force."
>
> **JOVE:** *(Procures [[Charm]].)* "What do you see?"
>
> **THALIA:** "I see... Your hand… [Jokingly.] I see The mark He granted her and that she shares with us."
>
> **JOVE:** "Is... is that it? All you get from it?"
>
> **THALIA:** "The Saint of Stars. Hm..." *(Concentrates on Charm.)* "Ave Imperator."
>
> **THALIA:** "Her prayers came to them in simple dreams. It was deviation from the doctrine they knew, from what was preached, but it warmed their souls. It did not stay simple though… They looked around and sought Justice but few had the Courage to march forth... Something saw this and subverted them."
>
> **THALIA:** *(Returns Charm.)* "These were forged by the first who believed on Finisculum -- they made her gifted mark to hold alongside the Aquila. It’s a prayer icon, essentially. A very good one."
>
> **JOVE:** "A very good one, huh?" *(Chuckles.)* "Do her prayers reach you, Sister? Do you hear or see her?"
>
> *(RECORD: Background noise -- [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] and [[Evodine]]’s voices.)*
>
> **THALIA:** *(Addressing Evodine and Lucky.)* "Thank you, young soldier, Sister. Ave Imperator."
>
> **THALIA:** *(Addressing Jove.)* "Her prayers reach us all if we but listen. If you mean a vision or word... once. She called me from my stupor to do His work." *(Gestures to her surroundings.)* "And these are the fruits of my trials."
>
> **JOVE:** "And if I told you she’s contacted me... what would you say?"
>
> **THALIA:** *(Fervently.)* "Then you’d be wise to listen to what she has to say. You would be wise to follow the light she provides."
>
> **JOVE:** "Meeting you was part of her counsel. I think."
>
> **THALIA:** "I am merely one who follows her ways, it was likely not that simple. That said, I hope my words provided counsel. Is there anything else I can help with?"
>
> **JOVE:** "Earlier, you said some malicious force subverted the first followers of the Star Saint. Is there anything more you can say on that?"
>
> **THALIA:** "Without casting aspersions with no founding? I am afraid not. I only really have hearsay and what I’ve noticed. I cannot be certain of ghosts... But during the early days, the ones I’ve heard from others and saw briefly myself, some other thought moved through the crowds. Whispers on the fringes that spoke of ambition, change, and remaking of the old. I’m not sure where they came from but the source always seemed to vanish quickly -- but the thought was planted. That sparked the first few rebel cells, I believe. The ones who sabotaged the factorums, who made their strikes upwards in the hive, the ones who inspired a militant fire in the rest."
>
> **THALIA:** "One force I am certain of thought comes from the noble houses. Those militant factions that arose would not have made it far save for the mysterious find of ‘lost’ Enforcer equipment." *(Sighs.)* "That’s the equipment you see the men outside using, so much of it made it down to the under and lower hives. I don’t know why they’d do this -- but they did and the consequences were bloody fighting and violence as the most immediate means."
>
> **JOVE:** "Can you name any noble houses in particular?"
>
> **THALIA:** "Nay, I am afraid I cannot. It was all material from the enforcers. Though, there are only two major houses and the rest owe fealty to them. An investigation on both would be needed, I would think."
>
> **JOVE:** "Interesting. I think that is all I have to ask of you, Sister."

---

## II. SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

Sister Thalia gave me greater insight as to the nature of [[Charm]] and the Saint of Stars -- particularly, that the ideology which first rose was twisted by the Deceiver. This gave them the idea of open conflict. Then, the noble houses lit the flame by providing arms to the rebels which initiated Initeium’s civil war.
