## I. TRANSCRIPT

> **EVODINE:** "You heard them, the mutants. What are you doing with them?"
>
> **NEYOR:** "Don’t know that one exactly… Boss did, I think." *(Coughs)* "Boss said guys down deep in the underhive bought us to bring these things up. Dunno why."
>
> **EVODINE:** "Where were you meant to bring them?"
>
> **NEYOR:** "Don’t know much. But it seemed like we were meant to drop ‘em off for someone else to take! I can also show you a safehouse. Maybe you can find what you’re lookin’ for there?"
>
> *(RECORD: Paused.)*
> *(RECORD: Resume.)*
>
> **JOVE:** "You will now be a confidential informant. This is what you will use to contact us. In exchange, your life will be spared. Additionally, should you find yourself in harm's way, you can call upon us for assistance."
>
> *(RECORD: Paused.)*
> *(RECORD: Resume.)*
>
> **NEYOR:** *(In response to heretical association.)* "And we’re not heretical, don't bring the damn ecclesiarchy in on this! We just wanted the thrones."
>
> **ADAMI:** "Do the things in those cages not count as a perversion of humanity? Dealing with the corrupt. Complicit."
>
> **NEYOR:** *(Muttering.)* "Not like nobles or the church want ‘em sometimes either, whatever."
>
> **JOVE:** "Are you telling me nobles and the church have been in contact with these mutants? Purchasing them?"
>
> **NEYOR:** *(Shrugging.)* "Some people come down here and have us hunt mutants to buy. Always thought it was for the show burnings. Or they liked… extra holes."
>
> **JOVE:** "Do you know anyone by name?"
>
> **NEYOR:** "No, we dropped it off and if we really wanted to, we saw hooded figures. No marks."
>
> **JOVE:** "Damn. Right, then. You’ll show us the way to the safehouse."
>
> **NEYOR:** "Yeah, yeah. Alright, it’s to the north a few blocks, and then to the west."

## II. SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

As indicated above, a noble house of Finisculum Prime, or multiple, are creating a demand for mutants. Neyor also testified about Ecclesiarchy involvement. Underhive Gangs get their hands on them, which for now is unknown, and smuggle them up through Initeium.
