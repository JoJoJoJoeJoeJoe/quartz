## II. SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

In this interrogation, we have learned the name of one of Initeium’s enemies: Genestealers ([[Coraptor Hominis]]). Their purpose, as a subspecies to a greater whole, is to weaken and destroy the institutions of their host planets for the arrival of the main xenos species. The suggestion of Heretek involvement has been suggested, which ties together with the heretek lab I saw in the last operation given to me before the Inquisitor’s departure. Zendros’ discovery of a self-aware servitor, and Nu-Rho’s reaction to it, are crucial to understanding why we have discovered more servitors in remote sectors capable of higher-functioning feeling.
