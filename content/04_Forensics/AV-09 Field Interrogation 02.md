## II. SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

Isolde was contracted by an unknown man who spoke to her through a holographic projection. She successfully disposed of a crate filled with parchment containing ‘code’ that the gangers were holding in their warehouse, waiting for someone to come and pick it up. As discovered later on, we have since discerned the stolen crate contained phrases belonging to the Deceiver as documented in [[AV-10 Verispex Analysis]] (Item 01).
