# AV-06 FIELD INTERROGATION 01

> **INTERROGATION METADATA**
> **INTERROGATION RECORD:** “Brass” Cell Rebel, Thalm
> **LOCATION:** Old Rail Line System
> **PRESENT:** Acolyte [[Julian Jove]], Warband, Sister [[Ariana Joy]]
> **RECORDING DEVICE:** Dataslate

---

## I. TRANSCRIPT

> **[[ADAMI]]:** "Answer. Who do you search for?"
>
> **THALM:** "We search for--" *(Subject winces.)* "We were searching for some prisoner that escaped. He went through here and killed some gangers!"
>
> *(RECORD: Paused.)*
> *(RECORD: Resume.)*
>
> **ADAMI:** "Was your prisoner a Scion? A Sister? Was it even human?"
>
> **[[24601-331-Draeta|LUCKY]]:** *(Distant -- handing over a dataslate with an image of [[Iric Advanis]].)* "Pressing evidence, Sergeant."
>
> **THALM:** "I... I don't know. I was just sent to help the team find some guy." *(Nods to [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]].)* "Maybe, maybe he found something."
>
> **JOVE:** *(Receiving the dataslate.)* "That he did. These lot were after the Scion." *(Regards Thalm.)* "The pict is obviously of a nobleman, that’s not just ‘some guy.’ Avoid dissembling any further, please."
>
> **LUCKY:** "Sergeant, read the back."
>
> **THALM:** "A... Scion? W-we were paid a lot to try and track this guy down so we got attached to this squad. I don't know details though -- me and... and... Oh, God, Winny!"
>
> **JOY:** *(Quietly.)* "He hides more than he shows. Grief is real, but... oddly muted."
>
> *(RECORD: Paused.)*
> *(RECORD: Resume.)*
>
> **JOVE:** "What cell do you belong to? Is there a name you go by?"
>
> **THALM:** "It's Brass cell. Just a name though, we're all about the Underhive."
>
> **JOVE:** "What is the end goal with the Scion?"
>
> **THALM:** "I told you, I'm not part of this team. They just told me to help them find a guy. Probably ransom? I don't know."
>
> **ADAMI:** "Which areas have you covered and who gave you this directive?"
>
> **THALM:** "We cleared the tunnels. The ones you came from, just started here. My boss sent me out here, who else?"
>
> **ADAMI:** "Two things, then. Where can we find your boss and how would you like to meet mercy?"

---

## II. SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

From this, we have direct evidence the rebels and the gangers were after the escaped [[Iric Advanis]]. He tried to feign that he was from the Brass cell, but I discerned this to be a lie to cover for his colleagues.
