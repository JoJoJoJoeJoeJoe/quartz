## Operational Background
Zendros Vayle of (Planet Name) comes from humble origins. At a young age, he was witness to hereteks that pillaged his planet and his people. Orphaned, he went into the Schola Progenium and became part of the Adeptus Arbites. Sometime along his career, a Rogue Trader led to the death of his partner, Liza, which was the last mistake that Rogue Trader ever made before their life was terminated by a rogue Zendros (as a result, it seems he is on thin ice with his superiors). His duties have since brought him to Finisculum Prime, in the interest of restoring the planet's ability to meet the tithe. Our collaboration is out of mutual interest, for if our work is to succeed, then Finisculum Prime will be able to resume its production.

## Assessment & Directives

> **PSYCHOLOGICAL & LOYALTY EVALUATION**
> Vayle suffered sub-clinical depression that has impacted his overall mood, but has little to no impact on his performance as an Arbitrator. This depression of his is owed to the loss of his partner, Liza. Other than that, I have observed no impairments in his cognition or behavior. He is, to me, a competent ally and liaison for Arbites and Inquisitorial collaboration.
> 
> A recent development, and perhaps not permanent, is Vayle's change in demeanor. He appears to be at peace with Liza's death and appears less interested in indulging his anger. Additionally, he taken to speaking with the accent from his homeworld, being truer to himself. He says this is owed to a dream he had, in which he had an epiphany that Liza would want better for him. I am befuddled by this improvement in mood, and hope that it endures – for the Zendros's sake.
> 
> Examining his personality, I see a man who scorns those with the appearance of lex-breakers (or are lex-breakers) but is relatively pleasant to all others. When he is not called upon to act as an Arbitrator, he is nonchalant and relaxed. Surprisingly, though, he's demonstrated a trivial amount of pettiness. And, dare I say, he can be prone to pouting.
> 
> He appears to be generally amiable with [[Evodine]] and [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]], and reciprocates a sibling-like regard with [[Adami]]. He and I have debated on the appropriate course of action every now and then, but our relationship remains professional and fairly affable. Granted, he is a later addition to our group, but he has not been the cause of much conflict – if any – amongst the retinue.
> 
> His loyalty is to the Lex Imperialis and the Emperor, as it is to the memory of Liza.
> 
> On the subject of malign influence or insanity, I haven't observed anything particularly worrisome – save the sudden change of mood. I'll keep an eye on that, but I assume it must have been one hel of a dream.
