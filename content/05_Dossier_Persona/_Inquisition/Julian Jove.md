## Operational Background
I initiated my training in the Scholastica Psykana on Terra at age fourteen, taught in the ways of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica. I wielded the strength of the Telepathica's schools of biomancy and pyromancy, this is true, but my main gifts was for Sanctic Daemonology. For this reason, I became a specialist of the Astra Militarum — a Primaris Psyker who climbed his way up to the rank of Savant-Warrant. I was deployed multiple times on Cadia to quell heretics, sorcerers, and daemons, and my deployments only increased when the war broke out. At Cadia's Ruin, I held enough of the planet together long enough for my regiment to get to the last transport; in doing so, my soul "burned up", and I could no longer call upon the Warp as I once did. I have drifted from assignment to assignment ever since, and eventually fell into your service – likely on account of my martial aptitude and knowledge of the Immaterium. My tenure with you lasted six months before you decided to cut me loose and leave me to tend to some "unimportant" leads. Now, I've plunged back into the bloody work of the Inquisition, and I do not seek to escape its service.
