## Operational Background
Segmentum Obscurus Command assigned Guardsman 24601-331-Draeta, Penitent Son of Krieg, to me from the 1313th Regiment. He is a four-year veteran of the Astra Militarum and its several fronts and conflicts. Owing to his latent psychic talent and aptitude for clairvoyance, 24601-331-Draeta survived perilous encounters on account of 'luck', which earned him his name Lucky. By my approximation, Lucky's psychic assignment would have fallen on Mu (M) prior to our meeting; however, his burgeoning powers have grown at least to Iota (I), making a return to the Militarum inadvisable. I have taken 24601-331-Draeta on as an apprentice, to add more psychic potential to Inquisitor Somtum's resources.

## Assessment & Directives

> **PSYCHOLOGICAL & LOYALTY EVALUATION**
> Lucky is a dutiful and resolute soldier of the Imperium through and through. True to his Krieg heritage, he is keen on being efficient and appropriate to protocol; however, he is becoming adapted to the discretion and subtlety required of your work, my lord. Regarding his loyalties, Lucky perceives me to be his highest ranking commander (besides you) and follows my commands without hesitation. Aside from myself, he has found a companion in Evodine whose previous status as a Dialogus Sororitas – and as a Hospitaller serving in a shared conflict of theirs – has endeared her to him.
> 
> In anticipation of any concerns regarding 24601-331-Draeta's sanity and purity of soul, the latter portion of his psychological assessment will be directed to his qualities as a prospective psyker for the Inquisition.
> 
> 	24601-331-Draeta's predilections lead him to the complete eradication of Chaos upon discovery, dismissing immediate concern of heretical temptation. So, too, is he appropriately fervent in his faith and commitments to the Emperor and His institutions, providing a rigid mentality to protect from exter-immaterial influence. One of his most promising aspects is the famed Krieg discipline which he exhibits. His conditioning from childhood has hardened his resolve and assists him in enduring the Warp's psionic pressure. One other thing of note: 24601-331-Draeta's innate psychic rank is comparatively lesser, reducing concerns of him falling to the maddening touch of the Immaterium. For these reasons, I am confident I can pass along the teachings of the Astra Telepathica to 24601-331-Draeta and forge him into the equivalent to my sanctioned siblings.
