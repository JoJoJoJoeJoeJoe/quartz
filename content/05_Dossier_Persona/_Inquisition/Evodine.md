## Operational Background
Evodine, of Finisculum Prime, hails from the Dialogus Sisters in Initeium. To my knowledge, she was once a Hospitaller and served briefly in armed service. I know little of her history, other than she's from Initeium's lower hives and – when orphaned – was taken under the protection and tutelage of the Ecclesiarchy. While she is capable of medicae, Evodine's speciality is in academia and forbidden knowledge pertaining to psykers and sorcery. In the recent past, she came into contact with a strange tome that presumably cursed her soul and opened it to the Warp, making her the subject of her studies: a psyker.

## Capabilities & Wargear
* **Primary Skillset:** Evodine's use in combat is consistently oriented to control and support. Her main use, however, is for intellectual challenges and her telepathic ability aids in interrogations and espionage.
* **Issued Equipment:** Flak Armor.
* **Special Approvals:** None.

## Assessment & Directives

> **PSYCHOLOGICAL & LOYALTY EVALUATION**
> Disarray, that's the word I would use to describe Evodine's mental state. All her life, she was conditioned to scorn the witch, and now she's part of the scorned. Overnight, she became the object of ire for many in the Imperium and lost everything familiar to herself, on top of being beset upon by the forces of the Immaterium. Her case is a tragic one for any, let alone a Sister. Unlike Lucky, her innate connection to the Warp is strong and has begun to test her sanity. (Insert assignment ranking here).
> 
> By my judgement, I do not believe Evodine holds much faith left in the Emperor. If she once saw Him as her beneficent God, she does not any longer, and has come to judge and critique the Imperium's institutions for being cruel. She is overly merciful to opponents/threats and rebukes allies if they differ from her in moral judgement; however, she is quick to shy away from or bend in the face of authority, fearful of detection. I've also noticed that she is remarkably paranoid and prone to suspicion, likely owed to the font of anxiety pouring from her soul. Lastly, her social clumsiness is owed due to a mixture of naivety (she is young), anxiety, and abysmal self-regard.
> 
> Assessing her loyalties yields nothing concrete; she sides with her common man and does not observe any fealty to the Imperium's institutions. However, one person she admires is [[Thalia|Sister Thalia]] – located in the Stricken Quarter, Underhive.
> 
> Befitting a talented rogue psyker, she is the most troublesome of my retinue and an ever persistent toothache. While this has been a scathing review of her behavior and personality thus far, she is not without grace. Evodine is truly compassionate for the people of the Imperium and seeks their well-being whole-heartedly. She is headstrong, like the other Sororitas I've known, which sees her through a lot of situations where others would falter. So, too, is she earnest – she isn't quick to lie, and I doubt she'd be able to give you an answer you wouldn't know whether to trust or disregard. I have observed her resolve grow over time, and I see that she is growing more and more resilient against the Warp's influences. While rough around the edges, Evodine is not without potential or value.
> 
> Note: I have observed, in the Empyrean, two contesting influences. I see the allure of chaos calling to Evodine, but in equal measure I see His grace reaching out to her. I believe this to be a sign of His providence, my lord.
> 
> In respect to her relationships within the retinue, she is very fond of [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] and cares for him deeply (holds a superficial romantic infatuation for him), appears mildly receptive to [[Zendros Vayle|Zendros]], and clashes frequently with and is disdainful of [[Adami]]. Recently, Evodine has warmed up to me and a stronger trust has been established between the two of us. More than that, she has voiced intent – and followed through – on seeking my counsel on matters in relation to her newfound curse. 
> 
> Analyzing her mental and spiritual stability, I sense that Evodine may not last indefinitely. Whether it be the fraying or her mind or the gradual corruption of her soul and body, there is a chance she wouldn't live for much more than 5-10 years in your service. Then again, few who serve the Inquisition do. Still, that's 5-10 years of service against the enemies of the Imperium. Regardless, I do not think she'd fall to chaos and become an agent of the archenemy as she is.
