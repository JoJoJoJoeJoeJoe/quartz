> **PERSONAL LOG:** ENTRY 03 – New Company
> **Date:** Day Two

---

This was, altogether, an exhausting and confounding day. 
Where do I start?

As recorded in [[Acta Veritas 03]], I have three additions to my one-man team: [[Evodine]] (the naive Sister-Psyker), [[24601-331-Draeta]] (the socially inept Kriegsman), and [[Adami]] (the equally socially inept and fanatical Deathcultist Assassin). I have no clue what [[Evodine]]’s deal is, I can’t shake [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky (24601-331-Draeta)]] loose without bringing unwanted attention to myself, and [[Adami]] claims it is her mandated prerogative to continue this mission. I would have forcibly thrown them off my trail, if not for the fact that their utility outweighs their drawbacks.

. . .

Perhaps I am being too crotchety. I ran into [[Aurora Harpina|Lieutenant Harpina]], my former Commanding Officer, and it has since soured my mood. [[24601-331-Draeta|Lucky]] tipped her off that I’ve been engaging in Inquisitorial work, and now I can’t predict how that’s going to bite me in the ass. Then there was the fiasco the company had with her, while I was away, which I do not care to repeat for the sake of my sanity.

I need to sleep. There’s another long day waiting for me.
