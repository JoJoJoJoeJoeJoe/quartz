> **PERSONAL LOG:** ENTRY 04 – His Touch
> **Date:** Day Three

---

I heard her voice, [[Inquisitor Somtum|Inquisitor]]. In front of [[Samson|Journeyman Samson]]’s shrine, where I asked for counsel, she spoke to me the words I have recorded below.

		“Seek His Hope. Don’t let it go.
	
		“Be not guided by the voices of your failings. Take to your own voice, take to Hope.
		
		“Let it guide.”

Once I departed from the shrine, I noticed [[Charm]]’s corporeal form was far more polished than it’d been before my prayer. This small blessing slipped past my attention at the moment, but it is not lost on me as I write this now.

My conversation with [[Thalia|Sister Thalia]] comes to mind as I ruminate on this day. She heard the [[Saint of Stars|Saint]]’s call when she was – in her words – a stupor to do His work. This parallels my case, for I first felt her presence when I wandered adrift in the [[Scions of Hope Church]]. In addition to that, the Sister told me the [[Saint of Stars|Star Saint]] spoke to her first disciples through simple dreams, akin to the one I experienced when I first encountered [[Charm]].

When Cadia broke, I broke with it. The forces of the Empyrean I commanded all but sundered my soul and I lost the powers I long since thought He gifted me. None of the meditations worked, I could no longer see His light or hear its presence, and I could not even wield an iota of the power I possessed. For a long time, I thought He abandoned or discarded me for being dysfunctional, failing him, or both. 

Perhaps I’ve looked at my fall the wrong way, perhaps I’ve been irrational. The planet itself shattered and when the Warp flooded in I held back oblivion long enough for my platoon to get to the last transport. Such a feat would not have been possible if not for His intervention. Even then, it’s not fair of me to blame Him for not protecting me from the consequences of my actions. I knew what would happen if I took so much from the Warp, I knew. I think the loss of my gift, the realization that I’ve only ever been human and not some predestined saint, was too humiliating for me to confront, too painful to the prideful man I once was – the prideful, arrogant man I can still be.

I am left to ask myself, was I ever really broken? Did I fall, or did I only perceive it that way? I think I know the answers to these questions, but I cannot believe them. Though I might not be able to see it how I once did, I will search for His light by the counsel of the [[Saint of Stars|Star Saint]].
