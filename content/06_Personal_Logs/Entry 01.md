> **PERSONAL LOG:** ENTRY 01 – Retirement
> **Date:** Day One

---

After I returned from my previous assignment, I discovered the headquarters here to be devoid of any personnel, deprived of most equipment, with only this dataslate left behind for me. On it, a message from the [[Inquisitor Somtum|Inquisitor]]: my tenure in the [[_Inquisition|Inquisition]] has come to a premature end – a retirement. The Inquisitor cited my emerging disdain for the merciless acts of the Inquisition as grounds for dismissal, and cited personal sentimentality as to why he spared my life. Knowing I am not one to be idle, he entrusted me with leads that were unimportant enough for his warband to look into.

In the [[_Inquisition|Inquisition]]’s wake, I am removed from my Regiment, the Warband, and deprived of purpose. I am left to interrogate and wrestle with the weakness of my character, and question how I have fallen so. Like so much else in my life, there are no clear answers and no forged paths ahead. All I have left, now, are the leads [[Inquisitor Somtum]] has left behind for me. I will see them through and exhausted until answers are found.

Damn you, [[Inquisitor Somtum|Inquisitor]], for your mercy.
