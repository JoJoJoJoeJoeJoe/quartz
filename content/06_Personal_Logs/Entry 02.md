> **PERSONAL LOG:** ENTRY 02 – Strange Dream
> **Date:** Day Two

---

When I awoke within that dream, the church held the same beauty and majesty of my homeblock. Instead of being hollowed out by fire, the church was immaculately well kept and the starlight was abundant. It lacked the clinical sensation of other churches I have visited, but was maintained by loving hands. There was a peace there I had not felt since my youth, not since I basked in the care and comfort of Grandpa Joko (Jericho Jove).

I am not the man of faith I once was. Since the fall of Cadia, the ruin of my powers, the gradual weakening of my mind and character, and the [[Inquisitor Somtum|Inquisitor]]’s revelation, my paradigm has shattered. For a while, my purpose was to destroy what I was guided to, and then it was to serve the [[_Inquisition|Inquisition]] which I have failed. I realize, only now, that I have squandered an opportunity to reach for something more. Yet last night, when I dreamt within the church, I was offered purpose again.

[[Charm]] told me that I have been selected personally by the [[Saint of Stars]], but for what purpose I do not know. Since [[Charm]] is mute, it’s not like I could have asked. Yet, when I asked them why I had been selected, [[Charm]] said it was the content of my character. However, everything within me screams weakness - I cannot ascertain what qualities the Star Saint is seeking.

Is this His way of reaching out to me?
Am I forgiven for failing Him at Cadia?
I don’t understand.
I don’t get it.

What I failed to record in [[Acta Veritas 02]] were the lines of scripture that I could make sense of in the dream. I do not know where else to put them, and so I have placed them here.

[Star Saint Litany #1] “O' [[Saint of Stars]], hear us and grant us your blessing. Let our chance be true, let us attempt the great work to be better than what we are. Let us not be man the broken, the frightened, or evil. Let us be as He would have wanted and be man the whole, the brave, and the good.”

[Star Saint Litany #2] "Let us follow in your footsteps and work and fight for the betterment of man, beyond the reach of the alien, beyond the thirst of evil, for you, for Him, and like you fought for, for our fellow man.”
